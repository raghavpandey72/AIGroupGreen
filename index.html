Valuation/Inspection of Property

Our company provides comprehensive valuation and inspection services to accurately assess the true worth and condition of various properties. Leveraging over 30 years of expertise, our Managing Director leads a team of skilled professionals in conducting meticulous inspections. We evaluate structural integrity, construction quality, and overall condition of residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Our detailed reports meet all insurance and legal requirements, ensuring you have a clear understanding of your property's value and state.

General Insurance Services

Our company specializes in a diverse range of insurance claims, including motor accident claims for OD, TPBI, and TPPD. We handle engineering claims, burglary claims, and business interruption claims like LOP and MLOP. Our services also include managing claims for deterioration of stock, shopkeeper's packages, and domestic insurance needs. We offer comprehensive coverage for workmen compensation and personal accident claims, ensuring your business and employees are fully protected.

Liability Insurance Claims

Our company expertly handles a range of liability insurance claims, including compensation for third-party motor accident claims, both on a fault and no-fault basis. We manage claims related to professional indemnity, public liability, product liability, employers' liability, and director's liability. Our experienced team ensures fair assessment and swift resolution of all liability claims, protecting your business reputation and financial interests.

Life Insurance Claims

Our company offers expert handling of life insurance claims, ensuring beneficiaries receive the support and compensation they are entitled to during challenging times. With over three decades of experience, our team provides thorough and compassionate assistance in processing claims, handling all necessary documentation and verification procedures with sensitivity and professionalism.

Identification and Handwriting

Our company specializes in advanced identification and handwriting analysis to uncover the authenticity of various documents. We address model forgery, traced forgery, and forgery by impersonation with precise forensic techniques. Our experts detect block forgery and validate embossing seals. Utilizing cutting-edge forensic methods, we provide conclusive evidence for legal proceedings and insurance investigations, helping establish the true origin of questioned documents.

Fingerprints

Our company provides comprehensive fingerprint analysis services, encompassing latent fingerprints, chance prints, inked prints, block prints, palm prints, foot prints, and shoe prints. We utilize advanced techniques like poroscopy and scale photography. Our forensic experts can identify individuals from partial prints and analyze ridge characteristics to provide definitive identification for criminal investigations and insurance fraud cases.

Modus Operandi Analysis

Our company excels in analyzing the modus operandi of criminal activities through meticulous examination of crime scenes. We investigate entrance and exit routes, analyze tool marks, and conduct site inspections based on the principles of exchange. This systematic approach helps establish patterns in criminal behavior, link related incidents, and provide crucial evidence for prosecution and insurance claim verification.

Legal and General

Our company provides expert legal and general support services for insurance and forensic cases. We specialize in recovery through subrogation, court appearances as witnesses, and detailed statement-taking. Our legal team offers comprehensive litigation support, prepares expert reports, and provides testimony in court proceedings to support your case with factual, evidence-based information.
